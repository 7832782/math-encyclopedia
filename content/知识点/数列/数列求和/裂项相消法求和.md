## 核心思想

将 $a_n$ 拆成 $b_n - b_{n+1}$（或 $b_n - b_{n-k}$），求和时中间项抵消。

## 常见裂项公式

| 通项形式 | 裂项结果 |
|---------|---------|
| $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}$ | $\dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+1}$ |
| $\dfrac{1}{n(n+k)}$ | $\dfrac{1}{k}(\dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+k})$ |
| $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}}$ | $\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}$ |
| $\dfrac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)}$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{2n-1} - \dfrac{1}{2n+1})$ |

## 典型例题

**例**：求 $\sum_{k=1}^n \dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}$

> $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)} = \dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+1}$  
> $S_n = (1-\dfrac{1}{2}) + (\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3}) + \dots + (\dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+1})$  
> $= 1 - \dfrac{1}{n+1} = \dfrac{n}{n+1}$
